### About me

I’m a high school student who likes building small tools and figuring out how things work. A lot of my tinkering starts with something on my Linux setup that I want to fix or automate.

### What I work on

- **Python** for trying out ideas and turning them into small utilities.
- **Linux + Bash** for automating repetitive tasks and tweaking my setup.

### How I debug

I try to reproduce the problem, check the logs, and make the smallest change I can verify. My favorite part is figuring out why the fix worked.
